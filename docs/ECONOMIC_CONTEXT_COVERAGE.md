## What the 95 price countries have

Of the 95 countries in the price artefact, **94 appear in the IMF datasets** (the State of Palestine, PSE, does not:
the IMF publishes no CPI or exchange rate series for it under that code, so every economic-context statement for
PSE is reported as "no series for this country"). For those 94:

| Usability (current series only) | Countries |
|---|---:|
| Headline CPI, food CPI and exchange rate all current ("full") | 38 |
| Headline CPI and exchange rate, no current food CPI | 30 |
| Headline CPI only | 12 |
| Exchange rate only | 6 |
| Nothing current | 8 |

So the complete macro comparison (general inflation, food inflation and the exchange rate) is possible for
**38 of 95** price countries, and **68 of 95** have at least a headline CPI and exchange rate. The investigator
states, for every investigation, which of these exist and why a missing one is missing. "Not current" is not
"absent": a stale series is still shown, labelled with the month it ends, and a series that ends before the month of
interest is never extended or carried forward.

Food CPI is the thinnest input: across all 213 economies in the snapshot the IMF publishes a current food CPI
(division 01, food and non-alcoholic beverages) for 79, a stale one for 32 and none for 102. Some countries have a
series that exists but is empty in the source (Niger: 201 months, all empty). Those are shown as "published but
empty", not as zero and not as a failure of the investigation.
